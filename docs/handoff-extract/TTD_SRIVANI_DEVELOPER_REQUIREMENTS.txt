# TTD SRIVANI Booking — Developer Requirements and Handoff

**Prepared:** 12 November 2026 · **Timezone:** IST / Asia/Kolkata (UTC+05:30)

**Starting project:** Booking Control Share v2.2.0, Windows desktop app and clean source.

## 1. Objective and engagement

Diagnose and resolve the supplied app's repeated **“Cannot book as quota exceeded”** failures and interruptions in the SRIVANI booking workflow. Deliver a usable Windows app that completes legitimate bookings when the account, pilgrims and quota are eligible.

The owner has not yet secured a ticket through the app. Correctly timed clicks, a successful login, a donation receipt, an order ID, or an open payment gateway do not by themselves demonstrate that the issue is solved.

**Required acceptance evidence: at least one genuine, confirmed SRIVANI ticket booking on each of three consecutive calendar days, with a screen recording for each day's booking run.** Section 7 defines the proof required.

This project may be shared with multiple developers. Work in your own copy or branch. Do not assume exclusivity, commission work, or incur booking costs without an agreed engagement. The owner is willing to pay for a demonstrated solution; the development fee, test expenses and payment milestones must be agreed privately before work begins.

**Change authority:** you may modify, replace or redesign any part of the application, including its timer, browser engine, UI, authentication flow, architecture, request handling and network implementation. Explain substantial changes and provide the resulting source. Preserve the privacy and booking-safety requirements below.

An existing-donor entitlement must not be treated as requiring another donation without checking the applicable flow.

## 4. Manual booking instructions for a baseline comparison

These are starting instructions based on the supplied browser workflow and official public information. Authenticated screens were not independently inspected while preparing this document. The developer must first reproduce the current manual flow and update these steps if the portal has changed.

1. Use the official portal on a supported browser and a stable connection. Prepare accurate details for genuinely eligible pilgrims and obtain the owner's approval for the intended expenditure.
2. Sign in to your own booking-site account. Complete the CAPTCHA and any OTP or additional verification the live site requests. Never use another person's saved session without authorization.
3. Open **SRIVANI Trust Donations / SRIVANI Darshan**. Ensure the category is SRIVANI, rather than ₹300 Special Entry Darshan or another service.
4. Choose the correct current, existing-donor or advance route. Check eligibility and available dates. For the primary test, use the agreed current / same-day route.
5. Read the displayed release time, quota and instructions. Check which steps are available before release and which are gated until release. Do not assume a preparation-stage Continue reserves a ticket.
6. Follow the donation/entitlement screen and enter the portal-required amount. Read the declarations and select only those that accurately apply to the account and pilgrims.
7. In the supplied app's baseline flow, an initial **Continue** opens the donation form; another **Continue** leads to booking-type selection; **Current booking** and its confirmation lead to pilgrim details. Verify this sequence live instead of assuming every button labelled Continue performs the same action.
8. Enter the pilgrim names, ages, genders, permitted ID types and ID numbers exactly as required. Complete any displayed verification. The launch notice described a four-person family limit and Aadhaar/mobile checks for current booking; verify today's category-specific rules. [S1]
9. At the portal's actual release boundary, use the enabled booking Continue control. Wait for the server's result. A click animation is not proof that stock was reserved.
10. If rejected, record the exact visible message, stage and time. Distinguish quota exhausted, not-yet-released, session expired, invalid details, throttling and network failure. Do not repeatedly create booking or payment requests when an earlier request's outcome is unknown.
11. If a valid booking proceeds, review the total, select a supported gateway and let the account owner complete authorized payment and any bank verification.
12. Return to the official portal. Confirm the final booking status in transaction/booking history and obtain the issued darshan ticket. A donation-only receipt or payment debit without a confirmed darshan ticket is insufficient.
13. Check the ticket's pilgrim details, darshan date, reporting time and official instructions. Save it privately. Redact personal details and ticket QR/barcodes from shared evidence.

## 5. Security features and constraints

### Officially documented controls

TTD's 13 May 2026 notice describes monitoring suspicious booking activity, blocking associated IDs and regularly strengthening its software. It specifically discusses misuse of rapid AI-assisted autofilling/copying. The developer must check the portal's current automation rules before proposing a live method. If the proposed automation is not permitted, use an allowed assisted/manual workflow or obtain permission. [TTD security notice][S5]

All changes must respect official quota, eligibility, identity checks, security controls and rate limits. The primary objective is reliable, legitimate booking for authorized users. No fake identities, account-limit evasion, broker/resale arrangement or fabricated ticket proof is acceptable.

### Implementation observations to revalidate

| Control / situation | Evidence available | Required handling |
|---|---|---|
| HTTPS and official-domain navigation | Public official portal addresses | Keep certificate checks enabled; verify redirects and the final payee. |
| Login and CAPTCHA | Existing browser client and previous app logs | Detect the real login form, allow legitimate verification, and confirm authenticated state before proceeding. |
| Account session expiry | Existing login redirects / recovery code | Reauthenticate and restore preparation state before submission; bound retries. |
| Authentication tokens and payment nonce | Supplied HTTP helper uses `ttd-auth-token` and `X-Payment-Nonce` | Revalidate the current flow; keep session values private and in memory. Never hardcode or share live values. |
| Throttling / anomalous-request rejection | Supplied error mappings include “Too many hits” and “Network Anamoly Detected” | Log the classification and respect the rejection/backoff. Do not infer a bypass from the message. |
| Quota and release enforcement | Quota-exceeded and not-yet-released states | Treat server acceptance as authoritative. Faster bandwidth cannot manufacture stock. |
| Payment and booking consistency | Existing gateway handoff and transaction flow | Check status after ambiguous outcomes; avoid duplicate orders, donations or charges. |

These observations do not establish a specific WAF vendor, CAPTCHA provider, queue algorithm or fingerprinting algorithm. Do not claim Cloudflare protects TTD merely because this project's old app-management service used Cloudflare. OTP requirements must be checked live rather than asserted for every login.

## 6. Engineering work and deliverables

The supplied app is a starting point, not evidence of a solved booking problem. It uses Tauri/Rust, React/TypeScript, FastAPI/Python 3.12, and browser/HTTP booking helpers. The clean v2.2.0 package has no app-account gate or cloud-management connection. Its existing automated checks and blank-start/shutdown checks do not prove live ticket-booking success.

Required work:

- Reproduce the failure and identify the rejecting stage. Determine whether the cause is eligibility, wrong quota, stale state, release mismatch, server exhaustion, queueing, validation, request ordering or another observed condition.
- Provide a root-cause explanation supported by sanitized timestamps, UI states and server outcomes. Do not describe all failures as an internet-speed problem without measurements.
- Measure relevant latency and scheduling behavior where useful. Any protocol, connection-pool, clock or architecture change must have an explained purpose and a before/after comparison.
- Implement a clear state machine. Recognize returned login screens, lost navigation, disabled controls and recoverable interruptions. Confirm the destination before retrying a known preparation button.
- Bound retries and provide understandable status/error messages. Unknown pages must produce a useful diagnosis rather than an unlimited wait or arbitrary button clicking.
- Audit concurrent request races and ambiguous outcomes. Once a booking/payment request may have been accepted, check its status before any retry. Preserve a way to stop the run and shut down only this app's own processes.
- Distinguish **prepared**, **awaiting release**, **booking submitted**, **awaiting payment**, **ticket confirmed** and **failed**. Do not label an open payment gateway as a confirmed ticket.
- Preserve empty account fields on distribution. Keep credentials, cookies, OTPs, ID numbers, tokens, live checkout data and raw traces out of shared code, logs and recordings. Do not reintroduce owner-linked remote management or undisclosed telemetry.
- Deliver the Windows executable/installer, complete modified source, dependency lockfiles, reproducible build instructions, a change summary, test results and the three-day evidence package.

## 7. Mandatory three-consecutive-day demonstration

Agree the test plan and dates in advance. Use eligible, owner-approved accounts and pilgrims who actually need the bookings; respect any repeat-booking restrictions. The account owner controls payment. Development fees and donations/ticket costs are separate; test costs require prior approval.

For acceptance, show **one or more genuine confirmed current SRIVANI ticket bookings on day D, day D+1 and day D+2, using IST calendar dates**. This means three separate daily recordings, not a single 72-hour video. Advance booking is an alternative only if explicitly agreed before the demonstration.

Each recording must:

1. Show the tested build/version and an IST date/time reference before release.
2. Cover the complete attempt from app launch/preparation through the release interaction, any recovery, payment handoff, and final official booking confirmation.
3. Be continuous for the run: no edited-out failures, substituted sessions, speed changes or reuse of another day's success. Any human CAPTCHA, OTP or payment action must be identified accurately.
4. Keep secrets hidden during capture or produce a redacted copy without removing the critical sequence. Never publish full ID numbers, passwords, OTPs, tokens, payment details or usable ticket QR/barcodes. Keep any sensitive original under the owner's control.
5. Show the official confirmed ticket and matching history entry. The owner must be able to verify the reference privately in the official account.

Use a daily evidence summary:

| Field | Required entry |
|---|---|
| Booking day | YYYY-MM-DD, IST |
| Build | Version and source revision / executable SHA-256 |
| Quota category | Current / agreed alternative; eligibility confirmed |
| Portal release and availability | Displayed values and observation time |
| Result | Official confirmed darshan ticket, or exact failure |
| Timing | Preparation, submission, gateway and confirmation timestamps |
| Manual actions | What the account owner completed |
| Evidence | Video filename, sanitized logs and privately verifiable booking reference |

Mock bookings, test-gateway screens, donation receipts, local success flags, isolated screenshots, three bookings on one day, or successes on non-consecutive days do not satisfy this requirement. Three days of clicking without confirmed tickets also do not qualify.

Record failures and outages honestly. If quota closure or another condition prevents testing, agree a new consecutive three-day window with the owner; do not silently omit a failed day. Server allocation cannot be guaranteed by a client app, and lack of stock is not evidence that a security bypass is required.

## 8. Submission and payment acceptance

Submit a short proposal containing the suspected cause, investigation plan, expected changes, required access, estimated delivery time, fee and test-cost arrangements. Use the clean source ZIP and your own development branch. Owner credentials are not part of the handoff.

Final acceptance requires: a reproducible working build; the complete source/change summary; privacy and shutdown checks; a supported explanation of the failure; and the three consecutive daily successful-booking recordings described above. Payment for the solution is conditional on the agreed successful demonstration and handover, not on a speed claim or a cosmetic app change. Exact commercial terms are to be agreed separately before engagement.
